# Templates

Templates are known-good starting points above the component level: whole pages and the blocks they are made of. Each one is built only from Atom63 components and tokens, and each is held to the same bar as a component: it renders in every theme and mode, passes axe, has visual baselines at a desktop and a phone width, and has no craft violations.

A template is a starting point, not a dependency. Copy its source into your product and change it freely; the product owns what it becomes. The templates live in `packages/templates` in this repository.

## What every template does

- **Keeps one primary action per view.** Other actions are `outline` or `ghost`.
- **Changes layout below `sm` instead of overflowing.** Tables become stacked rows, header actions wrap under the title, filter controls stack.
- **Never leaves an empty state without a next step.** It says why nothing is there and offers the action that fixes it.
- **Keeps the heading outline.** One `h1` per page, and empty-state titles take the level below the heading they sit under.
- **Takes every color, space and radius from tokens,** so it follows the theme, brand, mode and density.

## Readiness

**Draft** templates pass every automated check. A template becomes **Ready** after a design review in Storybook, because the checks enforce mechanics, not taste.
