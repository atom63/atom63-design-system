# Invoices

The invoices page of a small-business billing app.

- A table of about 25 invoices: number, customer, issue date, due date, amount and status (draft,
  sent, paid, overdue).
- Filters above the table: a search box that matches invoice number or customer, a status filter
  and a date range for the issue date. Show how many invoices match.
- Amounts are in US dollars and line up so they are easy to compare.
- Each row has a menu with "View", "Download PDF" and "Mark as paid" (only for unpaid invoices).
- When no invoice matches the filters, say so and offer to clear the filters.
- The page must also work on a phone.
