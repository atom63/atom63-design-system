description: Why this portfolio runs on a full design system, and what it costs to keep it.

---

A personal site is the one product where I am designer, engineer and client. It is also where I am most tempted to cut corners. Building it on a design system keeps me honest.

## What I get

- **Themes and modes for free.** Every colour comes from a token, so light, dark and four themes just work.
- **Accessible defaults.** Buttons, menus and dialogs keep their semantics without me re-learning them.
- **Content in MDX.** Case studies are Markdown with real components: steps, stats, figures.

```tsx
<StatCard change="34% fewer" label="Missed connections" trend="up" value="1 in 9 trips" />
```

## What it costs

Upgrades. Once a quarter I update the packages and read the changelog. It takes an hour, and it is the hour that keeps the other hundred working.
