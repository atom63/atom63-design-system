## Problem

Marigold Health is a network of eleven community clinics. A receptionist answered the phone, checked a paper rota, searched the patient record and booked in a separate calendar, often while someone waited at the desk. A booking took four minutes on average, and one in twelve ended in a double booking.

## Role

I was the only designer on the project. I shadowed reception at three clinics, ran the usability tests, and wrote the interaction spec the engineers built from.

## Process

<Steps>
  <Steps.Step title="Count the interruptions">Receptionists were interrupted every 90 seconds. Every screen had to survive being left half-done.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="One calendar, one search">Patient, clinician and room in one view, with the search always focused when the phone rings.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Drafts, not dialogs">An unfinished booking stays as a draft on the calendar instead of a modal that blocks the desk.</Steps.Step>
</Steps>
