A presentational keyboard-key cap, plus `KbdGroup` to lay out a sequence (e.g. ⌘ + K). Quiet and recessed — a muted surface with secondary text.

## Anatomy

- `Kbd` → `<kbd class="a63-Kbd" data-slot="kbd" data-size>`.
- `KbdGroup` → `<div class="a63-KbdGroup" data-slot="kbd-group">` with inline-flex layout.

## Contract

- **Sizes:** `sm` `md` (default `md`).
- Source of truth: `@atom63/ui-foundation` → `kbd/kbd-contract.ts`.

## Tokens it reads

`--a63-surface-muted` (cap fill), `--a63-text-secondary` (glyph), `--a63-border-subtle`, `--a63-control-radius`, and the control font ramp. No state, no new tokens.
