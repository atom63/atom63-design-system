The raised **action** archetype. Renders a `<button>` (or any element via `render`) styled by the `@atom63/styles` `--a63-control-*` recipe, reacting to the environment axes on the nearest `UIProvider` (mode / theme / brand / design-language).

## Anatomy

- Root `.a63-Button` — `data-slot="button"`, carries `data-variant`, `data-size`, `data-tone`, and (when loading) `data-loading` + `aria-busy`.
- Label `.a63-Button-label` — `data-slot="button-label"`, wraps children.
- Spinner `.a63-Button-spinner` — `data-slot="button-spinner"`, present when `loading`; label text is transparent and the spinner centers over the button.

## Contract

- **Variants:** `default` `primary` `secondary` `raised` `destructive` `destructive-outline` `outline` `dashed` `ghost` `link` `overlay` `glass` (default `default`).
- **Emphasis:** `default` is the neutral solid: high emphasis without the brand colour (a white play button on dark, a black Pay button). `primary` is the main action when the product's main action is in its brand colour. Use the accent for one or two cues per view; the main action may be neutral.
- **Floating:** `raised` (panel fill, primary text, soft shadow, no border) with `shape="round"` at an icon size is the floating button over a map or a light photo (back, locate, layers). Over dark media use `overlay`.
- **Tone:** `neutral` (default) and `muted`. `muted` applies to `ghost` only: the glyph and label rest in the secondary text colour and go to full contrast on hover, focus and press. Use it for secondary actions in toolbars, rows and card headers.
- **Sizes:** `xs` `sm` `md` `lg` `xl` + `icon-xs` `icon-sm` `icon` `icon-lg` `icon-xl` (default `md`).
- Source of truth: `@atom63/ui-foundation` → `contracts/components/button.json`.

## Tokens it reads

`--a63-action-*` (fill/foreground per variant), `--a63-control-*` (height/padding/font/radius/shadow ramps), and theme capability tokens (`--a63-control-gloss`, `--a63-control-highlight`, `--a63-control-shadow-active`, `--a63-control-shadow-tint`). Themes recolor purely by overriding those tokens — the recipe holds no literals.
