## Problem

Kelpline Transit runs 14 night routes in a mid-sized coastal city. Its app showed the day timetable all night, so riders planned trips on buses that had stopped hours before. Support tickets about missed connections tripled every winter.

<KeyIdea title="The question riders actually ask">
  Not "when is the next bus" but "can I still get home, and when must I leave".
</KeyIdea>

## Role

I led design from research to launch: I ran the ride-alongs with the researcher, owned the flows and the interface, and paired with two engineers on the timetable model so the design and the data agreed.

## Process

<Steps>
  <Steps.Step title="Ride along">Eight night shifts on four routes, with riders and drivers. The pattern: people plan backwards from the last bus.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Plan backwards">A planner that starts from "home by" and shows the latest safe departure, with the walk included.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Test on real nights">Three rounds with 40 riders, on the routes they ride, after 11 pm.</Steps.Step>
</Steps>
