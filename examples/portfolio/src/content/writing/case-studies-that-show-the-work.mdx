I review a lot of portfolios. The ones I remember answer four questions in order, and each project on this site follows them.

<Steps>
  <Steps.Step title="Problem">Who struggled, with what, and how you knew. One paragraph, one number if you have it.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Role">What you did, and what others did. "We" hides you.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Process">Two or three decisions and why you made them, not every sticky note.</Steps.Step>
  <Steps.Step title="Outcome">What changed for the people from the first step. Numbers, quotes, or an honest "we don't know yet".</Steps.Step>
</Steps>

## Media earns its place

Every image should prove something the text claims. A flow that shows the backwards planner is worth more than three hero mockups.

| Keep | Cut |
| --- | --- |
| The one screen that shows the decision | Device mockups on gradients |
| A before and after | Every iteration |
| A chart of the outcome | Logos of tools you used |
