Table takes `size` (`default`, `sm`) and `variant` (`default`, `plain`), and `TableHead` and `TableCell` take `sticky` (`start`, `end`). `sm` reads its row padding from the density contract. Item styles a row with `aria-current` or `data-selected` as current or selected. Toggle gains a `subtle` tone that shows a pressed state in the accent color with no fill.
