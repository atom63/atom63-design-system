Radio and Checkbox take `indicator` (`visible`, the default, or `hidden`): a hidden control stays focusable and announced but draws nothing, and its choice card shows a border and the focus ring. In a `size="sm"` Table, checkboxes and radios no longer add the layout margin that set the row height; their hit area is unchanged. SearchField takes `endAddon` for a control after the field. Avatar gains an `xs` size (20px).
