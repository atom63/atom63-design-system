Add Chart on its own entry, `import { Chart } from '@atom63/ui-react/chart'`: a small SVG line, area or bar chart (grouped or stacked) of up to five series over a categorical axis. It draws gridlines and axis labels from tokens, follows the container's width, shows a tooltip on hover and on the arrow keys, has a legend, mirrors in right-to-left text, keeps series apart by dash pattern in forced colours, and carries a data table of the same numbers that is visually hidden or shown with a toggle. It uses no charting library. The contract adds `chartTypes`, `chartSizes`, `chartColors`, `chartStates` and `chartSlots`.
