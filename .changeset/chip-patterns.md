Badge takes `dot` (`true` or a palette hue) for a leading category dot. ToggleGroup takes `variant` (`segmented`, the default, or `pills`) to draw its items as separate capsules for filter rows. Button gains a `dashed` variant, a pill-shaped outline with a dashed border, for add and filter affordances.
