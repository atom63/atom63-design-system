AppView takes a `rail` before the side panel, an `aside` after the main region (from `lg` up), and `topBarSpan="main"` to start the top bar after the rail and panel. The app-view contract adds the `topBarSpans` axis and the `app-view-rail`, `app-view-content` and `app-view-aside` slots.
