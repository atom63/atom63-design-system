Data and feedback components get their missing states. Pagination draws a disabled Previous or Next faded, mirrors its chevrons under `dir="rtl"`, takes `children` to replace the Previous, Next and More pages text, and its contract `linkSizes` narrows to `xs`, `sm`, `md`, `icon-xs`, `icon-sm` and `icon`. Table rows take `selected`, cells and heads take `wrap`, head and cell padding follow the density scale, and an overflowing table scroller becomes a named, focusable region. Chart gains `layout` (`grouped` or `stacked`, in the contract; `stacked` is deprecated), an empty message (`labels.empty`), tap-to-read on touch, real values below zero in the tooltip and table with a console warning, index keys, a short plot name (`labels.plot`), and whole-number ticks when every value is zero. ItemGroup is a list and its Items are list items (a link or button row is wrapped in one); interactive rows floor at `--a63-row-min-size`, have a disabled look, clamp their title and description, keep long strings inside the row, and mark selection in forced colors. Interactive Cards draw a focus-visible ring and lift, gate the hover lift and media zoom to fine pointers, read type-scale font sizes, and the cursor label uses on-media tokens. FeedbackState forwards native attributes and `ref`, takes `labels.noResults`, mounts a fresh alert for an error, wraps long titles, and its contract states are its kinds (the `idle` alias is gone). EmptyErrorDetail takes `labels` and mirrors in RTL. Skeleton keeps an edge in forced colors and sweeps toward the inline end. ChatComposer adds `submitOnEnter` (off on touchscreens) and `enterKeyHint`. The user ChatMessage bubble keeps an edge in forced colors. The shadcn bridge defines `--a63-row-min-size` and `--a63-action-disabled-opacity`.
