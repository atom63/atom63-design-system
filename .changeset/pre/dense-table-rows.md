Table `size="sm"`: a row checkbox, radio or button (a row menu, an inline action) keeps its own size and hit area but counts as 1rem in the row's layout, so dense rows are 33px with any of them (they were 37px with a checkbox and 41px with a 24px row menu). Phone screens keep their controls in the row. Sidebar menu items sit 2px apart instead of 4px, a 34px row pitch.
