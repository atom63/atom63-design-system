Extend the cross-renderer contracts for the new web treatments and add their SwiftUI counterparts. AtomTextField, AtomTextEditor and AtomFormField accept a filled variant; AtomCard accepts default, filled and elevated variants; AtomButton supports raised emphasis and muted ghost labels; AtomGhostToggleGroup renders quiet filter choices with native Button semantics; native Slider can take `atomSliderTone(_:)`; and AtomProgressView accepts the same accent or neutral track tone.
