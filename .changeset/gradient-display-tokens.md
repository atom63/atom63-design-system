Adds display type: the scale steps `10xl` (40px) and `11xl` (56px) and the text roles `display` and `display-large` (weight 600, strong 700; iOS draws them as `largeTitle`), with `text-display` and `text-display-large` utilities, plus an `amount-fraction` utility for a small, raised fraction in an amount. Adds gradients: `--a63-gradient-brand` (brand ramp 500 to 700) and `--a63-gradient-brand-subtle` (50 to 100), declared with the ramp so nested brands get their own, and `--a63-gradient-scrim` for text over photos.
